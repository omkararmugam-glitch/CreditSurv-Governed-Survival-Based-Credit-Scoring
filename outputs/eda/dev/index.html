Observations

Stated as observations, not conclusions: each line says what is in the data and leaves the interpretation open.

  • 21 feature pair(s) correlate at |r| >= 0.80, the strongest being open_acc~num_sats (+1.00), installment_to_income~loan_to_income (+0.99), num_actv_rev_tl~num_rev_tl_bal_gt_0 (+0.98), tot_cur_bal~tot_hi_cred_lim (+0.97), loan_amnt~installment (+0.95).
  • Columns with the longest tails by the 1.5x IQR rule: num_accts_ever_120_pd (22.2% of rows), delinq_2yrs (18.8% of rows), pub_rec (15.9% of rows).

  • By term_months, the default rate runs from 10.2% (36, 142,371 loans) to 16.4% (60, 57,629 loans).
  • By purpose, the default rate runs from 8.8% (car, 2,066 loans) to 18.7% (small_business, 2,202 loans).

  • By vintage, the default rate runs from 1.7% (2018, 43,862 loans) to 14.6% (2008, 137 loans).
  • Observed default rates fall for the most recent vintages because those loans have had less time to default (right censoring), not necessarily because they are safer.
